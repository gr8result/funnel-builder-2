Metforce channel Balustrade

Aluminium base shoe, glass with (or without) handrail

Common Applications:

Balustrade, Stairs & Voids, Pool Fencing

The Metforce Channel system uses a powder-coated aluminium base shoe to hold glass panels at floor level, delivering a true frameless glass balustrade appearance. An optional stainless steel handrail can be added for applications that require it. Glass specifications range from 12mm toughened up to 25.52mm laminate, and the base shoe can be surface-mounted or recessed into concrete. Rated from C3 to C5, the Metforce Channel is ideal for balconies, mezzanines, and commercial lobbies where uninterrupted glass is the design intent.
