Shower Screens
COMPLETE YOUR MODERN BATHROOM
Shower screens not only keep water inside the shower recess; it’s also a stand-alone design feature of the bathroom. Blending style, aesthetics, and safety our framed, semi frameless and frameless shower screens are the perfect enhancements to any bathroom.
ESSENTIAL (FRAMED)
Complete your modern bathroom with our easy-clean, stylish ' Essential ' framed shower screens. These framed shower screens are designed with convenience, aesthetics, and performance in mind, making them a popular choice for homeowners.
SIGNATURE (SEMI FRAMELESS)
Enhance your bathroom with our ' Signature ' touch. Blending style, safety, and practicality, our semi frameless shower screens add a modern finish to any bathroom. Made from 6mm safety glass that’s glazed to reduce mould and mildew, they are easy-maintenance and easy on the eyes.
FRAMELESS
The ' Frameless ' shower screen collection is our premium shower screen product. Modern,  and more than a little bit stunning, they are made from superior strength glazed 10mm safety glass.  Strong but elegant, they’re the perfect stylish luxury for any contemporary home.
Available in Regional Queensland only. Please confirm availability with a Bradnam's representative.
