The understated elegance of a Regency Frameless shower screen adds class to any bathroom. With no visible frame and seamless joins, there is simply no other shower screen as beautiful. Now available in a range of contemporary colours to provide even greater design expression.
Colour options
Let your personal expression shine as you mix and match elements and select from the stunning range of colour finishes to co-ordinate your bathroom vision.
Enjoy spending more time in your shower and less time cleaning it
