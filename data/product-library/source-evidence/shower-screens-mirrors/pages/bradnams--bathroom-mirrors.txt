Bathroom Mirrors
CREATE THE ILLUSION OF SPACIOUSNESS
Add a stylish touch to any room and create the illusion of openness and light with a Bradnam's mirror.
ESSENTIAL
Maximise feelings of spaciousness and add a stylish touch to your bathroom with our ' Essential' mirrors. Available in a choice of four contemporary, neutral frame colours, they are stylish, yet affordable.
SIGNATURE
Create the illusion of a frameless mirror while enjoying all the benefits of a framed one with our ' Signature ' mirrors. Designed with our unique micro-frames, they are sturdy, stylish, and oh so chic.
FRAMELESS
Add a touch of elegance and a generous dash of class to your bathroom with our frameless mirrors. The perfect complement to our frameless shower screens, they are also a stunning stand-alone feature.
Available in Regional Queensland only. Please confirm availability with a Bradnam's representative.
