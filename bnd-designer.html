All of B&D's Designer range of doors are sectional doors. Sectional garage doors are made from sturdy panels that run on tracks vertically up and horizontally across the garage ceiling. This type of door is also known as a sliding garage door, an overhead garage door or an "up and over".

    • Added Security

      A standard garage door is vulnerable when closed. B&D’s AUTO LOCK™ technology automatically secures your garage door with a heavy-duty bolt without manual intervention, ensuring your door is locked shut.

    • Added Protection

      Accidents can happen in a flash. That’s why B&D’s sectional garage doors feature a pinch-free design to prevent fingers or hands from getting caught while the door moves. With pinch-free technology, everyone is safe, no matter how often the garage door is used.

    • Safety and Security

      For extra safety and protection, B&D’s safety beam will pause or reverse your garage door if there is any obstruction, providing peace of mind and convenience for people with mobility or disability challenges.
