Panelift.

Panelift is B&D’s most popular sectional garage door, setting the standard for strong, safe and reliable operation.

Options and upgrades

  • 1. Windows

    Ideal for a Hamptons-inspired home, stylish Plain or Stockton windows can be added to Statesman and Grange profiles.
  • 2. Insulation

    9 out of 10 people use their garage for more than just storing cars. It’s worth considering the flexibility your garage space can offer. 


    Insulation acts as a barrier to heat flow and is essential for keeping your home warm in winter and cool in summer. The process of installing insulation in your garage door also means that the door is physically thicker, stronger and harder to penetrate and lift, making it stronger than a normal door. It also reduces sound transfer to other parts of your home, which is helpful if you have young children or a bedroom above the garage.
  • 3. Safety Upgrades

    Safety Beams help do exactly keep your family and precious items safe when your door is operating. The infrared beam detects when an object has blocked the path of the door closing, causing the garage door opener to automatically stop and reverse. If you have young children or pets, safety beams are essential for garage door peace of mind.
