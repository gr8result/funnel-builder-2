DISCOVER OUR RANGE OF GARAGE DOOR PRODUCTS AND ACCESSORIES

At Centurion, we offer a wide selection of products. We sell sectional and roller doors, garage automation, openers and remotes and innovative add-ons. Our doors cater to many budgets and styles and we offer customisable and luxury doors. Discover our range of garage door products and accessories.

Sectional Doors

Our genuine BlueScope Steel sectional doors provide the greatest flexibility in design choice. A sectional door is made up of horizontal panels which lay parallel with the garage roof when open. We offer a huge array of door options across multiple ranges and colours. Choose from Colorbond®, Timber Look, Sunshine Series and many other ranges.

VIEW SECTIONAL DOORS

Roller Doors

A roller door is a metal curtain that rolls up vertically in the opening around a circular drum wheel. We have a wide range of Colorbond® steel colours and styles, and our doors can be customised to fit most garage door openings.

VIEW ROLLER DOORS

Commercial Doors

Centurion Garage Doors has a long history of working with commercial doors. All our products are manufactured in Australia from quality local materials. A lot of research and development has gone into our roller doors over the years, and we take pride in knowing we are offering you only the best.

VIEW COMMERCIAL DOORS

Automation

We have a range of garage door openers, remote controls and accessories to suit your needs. Our automation products are made using the latest European design and innovation to suit both new and existing garage doors. Avanti Automation is the culmination of over 30 years of experience in the Australian garage door and automation industry.

VIEW AUTOMATION RANGE

Optional Extras

We have a range of innovative standard and optional product add-ons. If you live in a stormy coastal area, check out our Cyclone Protection page for safety features you can add to most of our doors. If you live in Western Australia, take a look at our unique Flush-Fit pelmets to give your door a completely seamless appearance.
