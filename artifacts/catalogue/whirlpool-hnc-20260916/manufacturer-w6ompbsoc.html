W-Collection 60cm 73L Pyrolytic Built-In Oven in Black S/Steel

The W Collection Built-in Pyrolytic Oven was created for those who want to be able to cook anything and everything with ease. With 40 different cooking combinations, you'll be able to whip up any dish you can imagine. And with the Ready2Cook feature, there's no need to pre-heat your oven - just pop your food in and let the oven do its thing. The Cook4 feature also allows you to cook four dishes at the same time without any of the flavors or aromas mixing. So whether you're a novice cook or a culinary expert, this oven is perfect for you.

Features & Benefits
  • 6th Sense Intelligence: No more guesswork. Simply pick your recipes, and let 6th SENSE cook them to perfection, every time. Up to 40 cooking combinations organized to fit your lifestyle and diet, for a full assisted cooking experience.
  • Ready2Cook: No pre-heating required. The Ready2Cook function uses a powerful convection system to quickly reach the right temperature, and completely eliminate the need for pre-heating.
  • Cook4: The Cook4 function provides a unique level of flexibility that enables consumers to cook up to four dishes at the same time without mixing any of the flavours or aromas.
  • Soft Closing Door: Slow-motion closing. Thanks to slow-closing hinges, the oven door shuts effortlessly, with a soft and silent motion.
  • Telescopic Smooth Motion Guides: This Whirlpool oven features soft-sliding shelves, for increased ease of use.
  • Pyrolytic Cleaning: Spotless cleaning at the touch of a button. Pyrolytic technology uses a high-temperature cycle to burn food residues into ashes, which can then be easily wiped away with a sponge. Heat-reflecting glass panels in the oven door ensure that the external temperature stays low, for added safety.
  • 5 Year Manufacturer Warranty: Guarantees peace of mind and value for money knowing that any product fault or defect is covered by replacement parts and/or a service call to ensure the product works as described.
