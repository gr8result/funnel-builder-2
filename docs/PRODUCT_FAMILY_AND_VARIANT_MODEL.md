# Product Family And Variant Model

The catalogue has three layers:

1. Quotation item template: the approved selection or allowance row with stable item code.
2. Product family template: the attributes the client must choose.
3. Builder product catalogue: organisation-scoped supplier, brand, range, product and variant records.

The first acceptance families are defined in `lib/product-library/genericProductLibraryTemplate.js`.

- Stone Benchtops: supplier, brand, range, colour, pattern, finish, thickness, edge profile, slab size, image/swatch and official link.
- Metal Roofing: supplier, brand, material, profile, range, colour, finish, thickness, image/swatch and official link.
- Bricks: supplier, brand, range, brick name, colour, texture, format, image and official link.
- Internal Doors: supplier, brand, range, design, construction, size, finish, glazing, ratings, image, price and official link.

Demonstration products are labelled `Demonstration Product` and do not contain verified commercial prices.
