# Area Template Selection Requirements

Every Area Type owns its own default requirement template. External areas must not inherit the generic Exterior template unless that is explicitly configured for a project.

## Balcony

Includes Floor Finish, Floor Tiles or Decking, Waterproofing, Balustrade, Balustrade Finish, Handrail, Drainage, covered Ceiling Finish, Lighting, External Power Point and Paint Finish.

Excludes House Numbers, Brick or Cladding, General Windows and General External Doors by default.

## Driveway

Includes Surface Type, Concrete or Paving Finish, Exposed Aggregate, Colour, Sealer, Expansion Joints, Drainage, Crossover and Kerb Interface.

Excludes House Numbers, Windows, External Doors, Eaves and Fascia by default.

Wet areas such as Ensuite, Bathroom, Powder Room and WC must include Floor Tile, Wall Tile, Feature Tile where applicable and Waterproofing. Bedrooms should offer floor coverings such as Carpet, Hybrid Flooring and Timber Flooring.
